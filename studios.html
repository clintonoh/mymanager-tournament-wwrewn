Studios

Professional broadcast studios and production facilities for tournament coverage and athlete content creation

What is Studios?

MyManager Studios is our comprehensive content creation and broadcasting platform designed specifically for tournament organizers and athletic programs. Create professional-quality live streams, highlight reels, and promotional content that showcases your events and elevates your brand presence in the competitive sports landscape.

Transform your tournaments into engaging digital experiences that attract more participants, sponsors, and viewers while building lasting connections with your athletic community.

Studio Management Features

🏢

Multi-Venue Operations

Manage multiple studio locations from a single dashboard with centralized scheduling and resource allocation. Track performance metrics across all your facilities to optimize operations and maximize revenue potential.

📅

Court & Equipment Booking

Streamline court reservations and equipment rentals with automated scheduling that prevents double-bookings. Allow athletes and teams to book facilities online while maintaining full control over availability and pricing.

💰

Revenue Analytics

Monitor studio profitability with detailed financial reporting and usage analytics for each facility. Identify peak hours, popular services, and revenue opportunities to make data-driven decisions for your business growth.

🔧

Maintenance Tracking

Schedule preventive maintenance and track equipment condition across all studio locations. Ensure optimal facility conditions for tournaments and training while minimizing unexpected downtime and repair costs.
